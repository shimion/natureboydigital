About the photographer

Meet David Matyis

David is a media professional, professional pilot and dad with over 30 years of experience in photography, film, broadcast TV and radio, and podcasting. His landscape and wildlife work has won several Telly Awards, national and international film documentary awards, and local photography contests.

He is a member of the Professional Photographers Association and has produced work for CBS, Entercom, Audacy and Fandango, as well as an outdoor TV series for NBC Sports.
