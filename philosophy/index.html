About David Matyis

Photographer, pilot and storyteller, with over 30 years behind the camera.

David Matyis is a highly experienced media professional, professional pilot and dad. He has over 30 years of experience in photography, film, audio, broadcast TV and radio, and podcasting. His photography has focused mainly on landscapes and, more recently, wildlife.

His production work has won many awards, including several Telly Awards, national and international film documentary awards, and local photography contests. He has created corporate video and photo productions for CBS, Entercom, Audacy and Fandango, and an outdoor TV series for NBC Sports. He is a member of the Professional Photographers Association and contributes to numerous landscape and wildlife photography sites.

A philosophy of landscape

For David, landscape photography is a philosophy, not just an art form. Nature mirrors human experience, holding stories of beauty, impermanence, growth and transformation. Each photograph captures both the scenery and what it stirred in him: an ongoing dialogue between the observer and the observed.

Slowing down to see

His work is rooted in mindfulness and presence. Understanding nature means slowing down and being fully in the moment, something often missing in today's fast-paced world. Rather than imposing a vision on a scene, David waits patiently for the landscape to reveal itself, so each photograph reflects the moment instead of manipulating it.

Honoring impermanence

Seasons, sunlight and erosion are always reshaping the places he photographs. That impermanence is central to his work, revealing both nature's beauty and its fragility. Photography becomes a way to preserve fleeting moments while accepting that they pass.
