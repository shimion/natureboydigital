Our Prints: Paper & Metal

Every photograph is printed to order in one of two museum-quality formats. Here's how they differ, so you can choose the right one for your space.

The fine art paper print

David's paper prints are giclée prints, from the French gicler, "to spray." Ultra-fine droplets of archival pigment ink, up to 12 colors, are sprayed onto fine art paper for exceptional detail, accurate color and smooth tonal transitions.

Moab Lasal matte paper

Every paper print is made on Moab Lasal fine art matte paper, chosen for its rich blacks, natural texture and glare-free surface. Paired with lightfast pigment inks, it gives an archival print that resists fading for generations when displayed away from direct sunlight.

Crafted with care

Each print is overseen from color profiling to final inspection, so it stays true to the original image.

The metal print

Metal prints are vivid, dimensional and almost luminous. The image is dye-infused into a clear polymer coating on treated aluminum. Light passes through the colors, reflects off the white base and returns through the image, giving remarkable depth.

Frameless and ready to hang

No mat, glass or frame needed. A hidden inset aluminum frame floats the print about ¾″ off the wall.

Built to last

  • Waterproof and scratch-resistant, great for kitchens, bathrooms and patios
  • Clean with a soft cloth or a mild vinegar-and-water mix
  • Lightweight and easy to hang, even at larger sizes
  • For bright, sunny spots, ask David about the optional UV-ink upgrade

Handle with care, especially the edges, and your print will last for decades.

Size guide

Each photograph is offered in the sizes that suit it best, and each photo page shows exactly which sizes are available. Some photos might need very minor cropping to fit certain sizes. A digital proof of your image will be emailed to you for approval before printing.

Need something larger or non-standard? Contact David about custom prints.
